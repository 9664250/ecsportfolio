      Click the Target Game:

      This game was made in processing. You must try to click the target in time. You may click the target as many times as you want, but each time, the target will get smaller and there will be less time. When the target gets smalle enough, the player is transported to a game-over screen. They must restart the game after that.

      Snake Game:

      Made a snake game in processing. The point of the game is for the head to reach the heart. When that's done, the player will gain points, which will appear on the scoreboard. If the goal is not completed in time, the player will be taken to a game over screen, and they will have to restart the game.
